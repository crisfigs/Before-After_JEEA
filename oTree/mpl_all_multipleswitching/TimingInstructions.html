Part 2: Video Order Decision - Instructions

To choose between the two orders, you will fill in a short table with {{ C.NUM_ROWS }} rows. Each row is one decision, in which you choose one of the two orders. The only difference between the {{ C.NUM_ROWS }} decisions is for which order you receive a bonus payment, and how high this bonus payment is.

One of your {{ C.NUM_ROWS }} decisions is then randomly selected and implemented. This means that you watch the videos in the order you chose in that decision, and you receive the corresponding bonus payment.

Notice that since each of your decisions can be selected, you should think carefully about each decision. Decisions 1 and {{ C.NUM_ROWS }} are each selected with a probability of {{ prob_extreme }}; each of the other decisions is selected with a probability of {{ prob_middle }}.
